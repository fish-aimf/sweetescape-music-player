Sweetescape, an alternative to spotify and independantly developed music player that stores your songs in your indexed.db

- This website requires an internet connection to function and stores songs as youtube urls in indexed.db

- Please don't sue me. this is just a passion project.

- New here? Press Alt + Shift + G any time for guide mode, which points at each part of the screen and explains it. The full <a href="/tutorial" target="_blank" rel="noopener">tutorial</a> has screenshots of every feature.

- Packed full of features basically almost everything can be double clicked/ rightclicked/ longclicked

- 8 months in the making.


Thank you for visiting and have fun. Consider staying. improvement updates every other day.
