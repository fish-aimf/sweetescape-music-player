How to use SweetEscape

This page goes through the whole player, top to bottom. Anything marked Hidden has no button or label, so you'd only find it by accident.

If you'd rather learn by poking around, open the player and press Alt + Shift + G. Guide mode points at each part of the screen and tells you what it does.

The screen

There are three areas. Music Explorer on the left shows what's playing and what to play next. The tabs in the middle switch between Now Playing, Library, Playlists and Lyrics. The control bar along the bottom has the transport buttons, the progress bar and the volume.

The row of icons at the top right, from left to right:

  • Moon: switches between light, dark and your custom theme (P).
  • Mask: Disguise.
  • Bar chart: Listening statistics.
  • Clock: Sleep timer (T). A countdown appears next to it while a timer runs.
  • Discord: Rich Presence. The dot shows whether it's connected.
  • Magnifier: Identify a song.
  • Download arrow: Downloads.
  • Cog: Settings.

Top left is your total listening time and the site's visitor count. Bottom right is the version number. Hidden Click it to read the changelog.

The two round buttons at the bottom left hide the control bar (H) and move the transport buttons left, centre or right.

Adding songs

Your library starts empty. There are four ways to fill it.

1. Search, then press Enter

Type into Search songs on the Library tab. If nothing in your library matches and you press Enter, it searches YouTube instead. Each result has Preview, which plays it in a small temporary player without adding it, and Add.

The "topic" button inside the search box adds the word "topic" to YouTube searches. Topic uploads are the official audio-only versions, which load faster and use less data. It's on when lit, and struck through when off.

Hidden Paste a YouTube link into the same search box and press Enter to add that exact video.

2. Modify Library

Modify Library, then the Add Song tab, takes a name, an optional artist and a YouTube URL. Paste the URL first: the name fills itself in, and Autofill Name & Author splits a title like "Queen - Don't Stop Me Now (Official Video)" into a clean name and artist. Hidden Hover over Autofill to preview what it will write before you click. If you don't like the result, Undo puts back what you'd typed.

3. Global library

The Global library button opens a full-screen browser. On the left is the current Billboard Hot 100 top three, refreshed daily, and Discover Something New, a few random picks with a Refresh button. Click a song to hear a preview. Hidden Click the "Billboard Hot 100" heading to open the full chart, where every entry has Preview and Add to Library buttons.

The search box on the right finds artists, songs and premade playlists. An artist opens a grid of their songs. A song opens a card with a listen button and an add button. A premade playlist is added to your library in one go.

4. Import a list

Modify Library, Import/Export, Import Songs takes plain text, one song per line, and whole playlists in braces. See Backup and sharing for the format.

The library

With the search box empty, the Library tab is a home screen with three shelves:

  • Favourites: a strip of every song you've starred. Scroll it sideways with the mouse wheel or a swipe. Click a cover to play it. Play plays them all as a playlist and All opens them as a list.
  • Playlists: your playlists as cover tiles, plus a tile for making a new one.
  • From your library: a shuffled grid of your songs. Shuffle reshuffles it and All shows the full list.

Hidden Right-click any cover in the favourites strip or the song grid to add it to the queue instead of playing it.

Searching

Search matches names and artists as you type. When only a few songs match they show as big cards, a few more get small covers, and long lists stay plain so they scroll fast. Press Enter to play the first match.

It also matches songs in other alphabets typed in Latin letters: eto finds Это, saranghae finds 사랑해, arigatou finds ありがとう, agapi mou finds Αγάπη μου.

Filters

Hover the funnel icon in the search box. Each row is Yes, Any or No: Favourite, Lyrics (has lyrics saved) and Downloaded (has a local file). Language guesses each song's language from the letters in its title, so you can show only Korean songs, say. Filters combine with the search text.

Song rows

Each song in a list has a star (favourite), play, edit and delete button. Small icons after the name show that it has lyrics, a local file or a star. Clicking the name also plays it.

Hidden Right-click a song's play button to queue it.

Hidden Drag an audio file from your computer onto a song to play that song from the file from then on.

By default the list is alphabetical with favourites on top. Settings, Additional can switch it to the order you added songs, or reverse it.

Editing a song

The edit (pencil) button opens Edit Song Details:

  • Change the name and artist.
  • The circular arrows next to the URL re-search YouTube for this song and let you pick a better video, for when the one you have is a live version or got taken down.
  • The share button copies a /song/… link. Pasted into Discord or most chat apps it shows a preview card.
  • Link local file picks an audio file to play instead of YouTube (Chrome and Edge only). The × unlinks it.
  • The lyrics box takes one line per lyric, each ending in a timestamp like [0:33]. Lyrics with no timestamps at all still show, they just don't highlight. Once any line has one, lines without a timestamp are left out.

Playback controls

The control bar, left to right: previous, play/pause, next, loop (repeat this song), autoplay (keep going when the song ends), the list button for the playlist side panel, and speed (0.25x to 2x). Click anywhere on the progress bar to jump there.

Under the song name is Next:, which tells you what comes after this. It takes the queue, skipped songs and loop settings into account.

Hidden Hover over next or previous to see the three songs on that side.

Hidden Right-click the song name in the control bar to copy it.

Now Playing tab

Once a song is playing, a Now Playing tab appears with a large cover and its own set of controls. Clicking the Now Playing card in Music Explorer also takes you there. Q cycles through the tabs.

Video mode and the miniplayer

Hidden U fills the window with the YouTube video. Press U again to go back.

Hidden I pops the player out into a small always-on-top window with the cover and controls, so you can keep it on screen while you work in another app. This uses Document Picture-in-Picture, so it needs Chrome or Edge on a computer.

If you try to close or reload the tab while music is playing, the browser asks first. Trackpad back swipes, mouse side buttons and Alt + arrow keys are blocked so you don't lose your place.

The queue

The queue is a list of songs that play next, before the playlist carries on. It doesn't have a button. Hidden Press Y to open it.

Hidden To add a song, right-click its play button anywhere: in a library list, in Music Explorer, in the favourites strip or the song grid. A note pops up at the bottom to confirm it. Right-click the same song twice in a row and it's set to play twice instead of appearing twice. Right-click a playlist's Play button to queue every song in it.

Inside the queue:

  • The search box adds any library song. Use the arrow keys and Enter to pick one.
  • − and + set how many times a song plays, up to 99.
  • Drag the grip on the left to reorder. Each row also has duplicate and remove buttons.
  • The header buttons are shuffle, add a stop block (playback stops when it gets there), add a loop block (the song before it repeats forever), clear, and close.

The queue is saved, so it's still there after a reload.

Playlists

The Playlists tab lists every playlist with its song count and rough length. A Favorites playlist keeps itself in sync with your starred songs.

  • Click a playlist, or its Play button, to play it.
  • The + button makes a new one.
  • Edit switches the buttons on every card to Edit and Delete.
  • Typing in the search box filters the list, and Enter plays the top match.
  • Hidden Press and hold a playlist for half a second, then drag it to reorder.

Editing a playlist

In Edit mode, the Edit button opens the playlist editor. Search on the left to add songs, drag the handles on the right to reorder, and Remove to take one out.

  • Hidden Click the playlist's name at the top to rename it.
  • The circular arrows at the top of the song list shuffle the order permanently.
  • Duplicates Allowed / Banned decides whether the same song can be added twice. Clear Duplicates removes repeats that are already there. Reverse Position flips the order.

Playlist side panel

While a playlist is playing, the list button in the control bar (or M, or Tab) opens its songs on the right. Click a song to jump to it. The search box filters the panel, the green arrows loop the playlist, and the expand arrows switch to a wider panel with covers that pushes the page over instead of covering it.

Hidden Long-press a song in the panel to skip it. It's struck through and won't play until you long-press it again or reload the page. Nothing is removed from the playlist. If you skip the song that's playing, it moves on straight away.

Music Explorer

The left column. O hides and shows it. From the top:

  • Now Playing: click it to open the Now Playing tab.
  • Recently Listened To: songs and playlists you played last. Hidden Click the heading for the whole history, where you can remove entries and set how many are kept.
  • Suggested and Your Picks: songs from your library to play next. Hidden Click either heading to reshuffle it.
  • Recently Played Playlists.

Click to play, right-click to queue. Settings, Recommendation sets how many items each section shows, sets 0 to hide one, and changes their order.

Lyrics

The Lyrics tab shows the current song's lyrics and highlights each line as it's sung. The three round buttons in the corner keep the current line centred, copy a karaoke link, and open fullscreen lyrics.

Hidden Right-click the Lyrics tab to list every song in your library that has lyrics. Click one to open it in the editor.

Getting lyrics for a song

If a song has none, the tab offers two buttons.

Transcribe lyrics opens the Lyrics Maker, which works in three steps:

  1. Setup: paste the words, or use Auto-Fetch to pull them from Genius. There are also buttons that search AZLyrics, Letras, Genius and Google for you. Then Prepare Lyrics.
  2. Record: press Start Recording, then Mark Line the moment each line begins. Redo and Skip fix mistakes as you go.
  3. Export: check the result, then Save to Song, or copy it.

Import subtitles as lyrics uses the video's YouTube captions instead. Auto-Fetch Transcript grabs them (pick a language if there are several). Otherwise, open the video, copy its transcript, and paste it in. Convert to Lyrics turns it into timed lines you can tidy up before saving.

Fullscreen lyrics

The expand button on the Lyrics tab asks which style you want, then hides everything except the lyrics, the bottom bar and the visualizer. Esc leaves, and the layers button in the corner switches style without leaving.

  • Verse: music-video style lines that roll upward.
  • Cards: each line is a card that rises and lands as the line starts.
  • Mirror: the cover in the middle, with lines trading sides.
  • Lyric Video: three lines at a time, the oldest swapped for the next.
  • Flow: the whole song scrolls past. This is the only style that works with lyrics that have no timestamps.

Karaoke links

The share button on the Lyrics tab copies a link that holds the song and its timed lyrics. Nothing is uploaded, the lyrics are packed into the address itself. Whoever opens it gets a karaoke page with the video, synced lyrics, a speed control and its own fullscreen mode. Save to Library adds the song, lyrics included, to their library.

Identify a song

The magnifier icon. Choose where the sound comes from:

  • Tab / System audio: share a browser tab or your whole screen's audio, for a song playing in a video or another app.
  • Microphone: hold your device near the music. Turn up Input gain if it's quiet.
  • Upload clip: an audio or video file.

Once there's a recording you can play it back and drag across the waveform to pick the best part. Identify selection or Identify clip sends it off. A match shows the cover and links to Shazam, YouTube Music, YouTube and Spotify, and the + button searches for it so you can add it.

Downloads and local files

The download arrow opens a small download manager with three tabs: Current song, Search library and YouTube URL. Add songs to its list, then download them one by one or with Download all. Files are MP3s.

There's also a stand-alone page at /download that does the same on a phone, without opening the player.

To play a downloaded file instead of YouTube, drag it onto the song in your library, or use Link local file in the song's edit dialog. Local files keep playing with no connection, and the visualizer reacts to their actual sound.

Listening statistics

Statistics are off until you switch on Track Listening Statistics in Settings, Additional. After that, a song counts as played once you've heard a third of it, or ten seconds, whichever is shorter.

The bar chart icon shows the last 30 days or all time: time listened, plays, number of songs, your top artist and your top songs. Click any song for its own history: plays per day or per month, its best day and when you last played it, as a chart or a table.

Switching statistics off deletes everything they've recorded.

Themes and glass

The moon icon (P) cycles light, dark and custom. Settings, Theme has the rest:

  • Surface Style: Solid, or Glass, which makes panels see-through over a blurred background. Glass has sliders for transparency, blur and vibrancy, plus presets.
  • Album Art Background: in glass mode, fills the background with a soft, slowly drifting copy of the current song's cover.
  • Background: your own image, or one of the built-in gradients, with fit, dim and blur. The image stays on your device.
  • Visualizer Colour: your theme colour, any colour you pick, or Auto, which takes it from the album cover.
  • Customize Colors: set all twelve theme colours by hand. Export Theme to Clipboard gives you a code to share, Import Theme takes one, and Saved Themes keeps several to switch between.

Visualizer

The bars at the sides of the screen. Turn it on or off, and pick one of six designs (Bars, Levels, Wave, Ribbon, Radial, Glow), in Settings, Additional. Magnitude makes it bigger or smaller.

It reads the real audio for songs playing from a local file. YouTube's player doesn't let pages read its sound, so for YouTube songs it moves in time with the music but isn't measuring it.

Sleep timer

The clock icon or T. First choose what happens when it runs out, Stop Music or Close App. Then pick a preset from 30 seconds to 15 minutes, type any number of minutes, or set a clock time. The countdown shows next to the clock icon, and Cancel Timer stops it.

Disguise

The mask icon. Tab disguise on the left changes the browser tab's icon and title to look like Google Docs, Gmail, Classroom, Desmos and about twenty others. It stays until you pick Original.

Page disguise on the right covers the whole player with a real website, a preset one or any address you type, while the music keeps playing. Esc or the × in the corner brings the player back.

Discord

Shows what you're listening to on your Discord profile. A browser can't talk to Discord directly, so this needs a small helper program running on your computer. The download link is in the dialog. Turn on Enable Rich Presence, and the three steps on the left go green once the helper and Discord are connected.

The right side shows what's being sent. You can overwrite the song, artist or picture, Reset to go back to automatic, or Send Now to push an update immediately.

Backup and sharing

Modify Library, Import/Export. Export Songs lets you export the library, the library with every playlist, or a single playlist. It's plain text, so you can keep it anywhere and read it.

Import Songs takes the same text. One song per line as Name, YouTube URL or Name, YouTube URL, Artist. Playlists go in braces:

Road Trip{
Take On Me, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=djV11Xbc914, a-ha
Africa, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FTQbiNvZqaY, TOTO
}

Songs already in your library are skipped, and commas inside song names are fine.

To share one song, use the share button in its edit dialog. To share a song with its lyrics, use a karaoke link.

Other settings

  • Recommendation: how many recently played songs to remember, and the order and size of each Music Explorer section.
  • Feedback: send a note to the developer.
  • Additional: ads (off by default), the visualizer, library sort order, and listening statistics.

Guide mode

Press Alt + Shift + G anywhere in the player. On a Mac that's Option + Shift + G. You can also open the player with guide mode on.

Guide mode has two parts. The tour dims the screen and steps through each area with a short note, including the hidden gestures on this page. Use the arrow keys or the buttons, and Esc to stop. Point and ask lets you hover over, or tap, anything on the screen to find out what it is.

The guide is downloaded only when you ask for it, and never cached, so it doesn't slow down the player for anyone who doesn't use it.

Every hidden gesture

All in one place:

  • Right-click a song's play button, a Music Explorer song, a favourite, or a cover in the song grid: add it to the queue.
  • Right-click a playlist's Play button: queue the whole playlist.
  • Y: open the queue.
  • Long-press a song in the playlist side panel: skip it for this session.
  • Press and hold a playlist card, then drag: reorder playlists.
  • Click a playlist's name in its editor: rename it.
  • Right-click the Lyrics tab: every song with lyrics.
  • Click "Recently Listened To": full history. Click "Suggested" or "Your Picks": reshuffle.
  • Click "Billboard Hot 100" in the Global library: the full chart.
  • Hover next or previous: the next or previous three songs.
  • Right-click the song name in the control bar: copy it.
  • Hover Autofill: preview the cleaned-up name and artist.
  • Paste a YouTube link into the library search and press Enter: add it.
  • Drag an audio file onto a song: play it from that file.
  • Click the version number: the changelog.
  • U: video mode. I: miniplayer.
